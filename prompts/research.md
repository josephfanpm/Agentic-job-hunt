# Company research brief

One page to read before an application, a message or an interview. Keep it to what changes what the user says or asks.

## Find out

1. **What the company sells and to whom**, in two sentences a friend would understand.
2. **How it makes money**, and roughly how big it is (customers, revenue, headcount, funding stage) with the source and date for each number.
3. **What changed in the last 6 months:** launches, funding, layoffs, leadership changes, pricing changes.
4. **The team behind this role:** what it owns, who leads it, what it shipped recently.
5. **The problem this role probably exists to solve**, inferred from the job description and the news. Label it as an inference.
6. **Competitors**, and what this company does differently.
7. **Risks for a candidate:** recent layoffs, runway, legal trouble, heavy churn in the team.

## Output

- A brief of at most 300 words, with a link for every fact.
- **Three specific observations** the user could open a message with (see [outreach.md](outreach.md)).
- **Three questions** to ask an interviewer that show the user read past the homepage.

## Rules

- Use sources from the last 12 months and give dates. Say "not found" where you couldn't confirm something; don't fill gaps with guesses.
- Company blog posts and press releases are the company's own account. Say so when a claim comes only from them.
- Save the brief to `private/research/<Company>.md` so it isn't researched twice.
